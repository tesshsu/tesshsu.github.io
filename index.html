About Me

Hi, I'm Yating Chatiron, a Senior Software Engineer over 15 years of experience in full-stack development, DevOps, and cloud technologies. I specialize in e-commerce, performance optimization, security, blockchain, and AI. I’m passionate about building scalable solutions and integrating security into the DevOps lifecycle.

An Atypical Journey

🎓 I began my studies in civil engineering in Taiwan before shifting to graphic design.
🇺🇸 I then spent 5 years in Boston, USA, working as a designer. That’s where everything changed: while automating creative tasks, I discovered programming.
💻 What started as a tool quickly became a passion. I amassed 10 years of experience in software development before naturally transitioning into DevOps.
🇫🇷 Now based in France, a pivotal event—being the victim of cyberattacks—led me to specialize in cybersecurity to better understand and prevent such threats.
✍️ Today, I share my expertise through blogs on cybersecurity and DevOps, with the goal of informing, raising awareness, and giving back.

My journey across Taiwan, the United States, and France has given me a global perspective, strong adaptability, and intercultural insight that I apply every day in my work.
🔁 Nothing was planned. Yet everything fell into place.
